# Face System V5

Open **Face → Open Eye Design Lab** to use the actual Algowzxd face preview, switch between Classic, Expressive, Anime/Comedy and Custom packs, choose a paired expression, or override the left and right eyes independently. Brows are independent and can follow the expression automatically or use explicit presets.

The lab's **Contact Sheet** button opens `/face/eye-contact-sheet`, a local visual review page that renders every exposed expression on the real character head and labels its intensity tier and eye style.

The black sunglasses accessory is a separate SVG layer. Its placement controls are saved with the project; **Keyframe On/Off at Playhead** creates or replaces a boolean keyframe on a normal editable timeline lane.

AI performance generation selects from the expanded semantic expression vocabulary, writes separate left/right expression lanes and separate left/right brow-preset lanes, and continues to animate gaze independently. Tier-3 extreme/comedic presets are reserved by prompt guidance for strong moments. AI never enables sunglasses.

Legacy projects are upgraded in memory with default eye, brow and accessory state. The classic asset library is preserved and selectable; uploads remain per-expression and per-side.
